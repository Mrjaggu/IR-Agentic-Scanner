Held-out evaluation

—

Each test quarter is scored separately against a training cutoff that excludes it and everything after it — predicting Q1FY27 trains through Q4FY26, predicting Q4FY26 trains through Q3FY26. That is the production setting: before a real call you do have every prior call. Scores are never averaged into a single headline, and the spread between quarters is reported as a first-class number.
